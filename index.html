Download & Install on Android

AKROO BM is a high-performance Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it directly onto your Android phone as a standalone app with its own home screen icon and offline support.

Point your Phone Camera to Scan:
http://192.168.1.106:8080
Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi.
1
Connect your Android phone to the same Wi-Fi network as this computer.
2
Open Google Chrome on your phone and go to http://192.168.1.106:8080.
3
Tap the top Chrome menu (⋮) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
✓
Done! The AKROO BM icon will appear on your Android home screen and launch fullscreen like a native APK.
